# TableroBlanco screenshots

[Español](CAPTURAS.md) · **English** · [简体中文](SCREENSHOTS.zh-CN.md)

These images show the app with demonstration data. They do not contain personal boards. The home screen, editor and dark-theme screenshots show version 1.2.1. Screenshots use the Spanish or Simplified Chinese interface; captions on this page are in English.

## Home screen

Local library, board creation, favorites, trash and template shortcuts.

![Board library in the light theme](images/inicio.png)

## Editor

Text, sticky notes and drawing tools on a canvas you can pan across.

![Editor with a welcome board and three sticky notes](images/editor.png)

## Templates by category

The catalog contains 57 editable templates in nine categories. Search the entire library and reuse personal templates.

![Learning category with nine templates and global search](images/plantillas.png)

## Dark theme

Change the appearance in Settings. Your choice is saved on the device.

![Board library with the dark theme enabled](images/tema-oscuro.png)

## Languages

Spanish, English and Simplified Chinese. Changing the language affects the interface and new templates while preserving content written by the user.

![Template picker with the Simplified Chinese interface](images/idiomas.png)
